Iced Latte

Description

An Iced Latte is a smooth and refreshing coffee drink made with espresso, cold milk, and ice. It has a balanced coffee flavor that is less intense than an iced americano because the milk makes it creamy and slightly sweet naturally. Perfect for summer mornings or afternoon caffeine cravings.

Ingredients

  • 2 shots espresso
  • 180 ml cold milk
  • 1 cup ice cubes
  • Sugar or sweetener (optional)

Steps

  1. Fill a tall glass with ice cubes.
  2. Pour cold milk into the glass.
  3. Brew 2 shots of espresso.
  4. Slowly pour the espresso over the milk and ice.
  5. Add sugar or syrup if desired.
  6. Stir lightly and serve immediately.
